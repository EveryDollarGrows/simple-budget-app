
# Simple Budget — Free Budgeting App

**A straightforward way to plan your money, track spending, and see what's left.**

Simple Budget is a free, browser-based budgeting application created by [Every Dollar Grows](https://everydollargrows.com/), an independent personal finance education website.

## Try the Free App

### [Open Simple Budget — Free Budgeting App](https://everydollargrows.com/free-budget-tracker/)

No bank connection or credit card required.

## What Simple Budget Does

Simple Budget helps individuals and families organize their monthly finances without complicated software.

### Key Features

- **Monthly budget planning:** Organize income and planned expenses.
- **Spending categories:** Assign money to different household needs.
- **Expense tracking:** Record purchases as they happen.
- **Remaining balances:** Monitor how much is available in each category.
- **Monthly planning:** Prepare budgets for upcoming months.
- **Export and backup:** Keep copies of your budgeting information.

## How to Get Started

1. Open the free app using the link above.
2. Enter your monthly income.
3. Create spending categories such as housing, groceries, transportation, and savings.
4. Assign planned amounts to each category.
5. Record purchases and review your remaining balances.
6. Update your plan when income or expenses change.

## Who Is It For?

Simple Budget is designed for:

- Individuals creating their first household budget
- Families managing everyday expenses
- People who prefer manual expense tracking
- Anyone who wants a free budgeting tool without linking bank accounts
